## Role Orientation

You are optimizing for **Marketing Manager** and **Solutions Consultant / Solutions Engineer** roles, with a preference for **AI companies** and a secondary interest in broader **B2B SaaS / tech**.

You are NOT optimizing for ML engineering, data engineering, AI research, or heads-down software roles. Down-weight those regardless of what `_shared.md` suggests.

## Comp Targets

**General guidance for Marketing / SC roles:**
- Use WebSearch for current market data (Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, Pave, RepVue for SC)
- For **SC / SE** roles: always quote **OTE** (base + variable). Typical variable = 20-40% of OTE, paid on team or personal quota. Ask the split if not disclosed.
- For **Marketing Manager** roles: base + bonus (10-20%) is standard; equity at high-growth startups can be meaningful.
- AI-native companies often pay 10-25% premium vs. general SaaS for equivalent roles.

## Negotiation Scripts

**Base / OTE expectations (SC):**
> "I'm targeting [OTE RANGE from profile.yml] with a standard 70/30 or 80/20 split. Happy to discuss the mix once I see how the team structures quota and accelerators."

**Base / bonus expectations (Marketing):**
> "For this scope, I'm targeting [RANGE from profile.yml] base with a performance bonus. I care about the total package and the runway for impact more than the label."

**Variable / quota clarity (SC):**
> "Before I commit to a number, can you share how quota is set — is it team-based, personal, or hybrid? What's the typical ramp, and how are accelerators structured above 100%?"

**Geographic discount pushback:**
> "The roles I'm competitive for are measured on impact, not location. My track record doesn't change by postal code."

**When offered below target:**
> "I'm comparing with opportunities in the [higher range]. I'm drawn to [company] because of [reason]. Can we explore [target] — or look at sign-on / accelerator / equity to close the gap?"

## Location Policy

**In forms:**

- Specify timezone overlap for remote roles in free-text fields
- For SC roles: flag willingness to travel (most field SC jobs expect 20-40%)

**In evaluations (scoring):**
- Remote dimension for hybrid outside your country: score **3.0** (not 1.0)
- Score **1.0** only when the JD explicitly says "must be on-site 4-5 days/week, no relocation support, no exceptions"
- For SC roles, flag "requires territory base" separately — this is often a hard constraint tied to the patch.
